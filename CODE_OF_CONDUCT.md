# Code of Conduct

## The Short Version

Don't be an asshole. We're all here to make AI systems fail less catastrophically. That's hard enough without interpersonal drama.

---

## The Longer Version

### Our Standards

**Behavior that makes this project better:**

- Constructive criticism of ideas (including the doctrine itself)
- Clear, direct communication
- Assuming good intent until proven otherwise
- Admitting when you're wrong
- Sharing failure stories without shame—that's literally the point
- Helping newcomers understand the philosophy
- Disagreeing respectfully

**Behavior that will get you shown the door:**

- Personal attacks or insults
- Harassment of any kind
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Trolling or bad-faith arguments
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Spam or self-promotion disguised as contribution
- Demanding unpaid labor from maintainers

### The Gray Area

This project has a specific tone: direct, occasionally dark, slightly punk. That's intentional. We make jokes about AI failures because gallows humor is a coping mechanism.

**Acceptable:** "Your model isn't lying, it's bullshitting" (in documentation about AI behavior)

**Not acceptable:** Personal insults directed at contributors

The difference is target. Mock the failure modes, not the humans.

---

## Enforcement

### What Happens If You Violate This

1. **First offense (minor):** Private message from maintainer, explanation of issue
2. **Second offense or major violation:** Temporary ban from participation
3. **Pattern of behavior or severe violation:** Permanent ban

Maintainers have final say. This isn't a democracy, it's a project.

### Reporting

If someone is violating this code of conduct:

1. **In public spaces:** Tag a maintainer or open an issue
2. **Private concerns:** Email maintainers directly

We take reports seriously and will not retaliate against good-faith reporters.

---

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues, PRs, and discussions
- Any official project communication channels
- Representing the project at events or online

It does not apply to your personal life, opinions outside this project, or how you run your own AI systems. (Though we do judge silently.)

## Questions?

If you're unsure whether something is acceptable, it probably is. We're not looking for reasons to exclude people. We're looking to build something useful.

Just don't be an asshole. That's really it.
